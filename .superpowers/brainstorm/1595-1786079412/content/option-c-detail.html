Panel Features Summary

📍 Position Slides in from right edge. P&L compresses. Uses CSS transform: translateX with transition: 0.3s ease
📊 Sorting Journal entries first (newest → oldest), then unposted bank tx (newest → oldest), separated by dashed divider
🔗 Doc links 📎 = invoice link, 📄 = bank statement scan link. Open in new tab or preview modal
🩷 Pink "Post →" Navigates to /bookkeeping?tab=statements&statement=BS-XXXX&highlight=TX-ID. Opens statement, scrolls to tx, yellow highlight animation
🎨 Highlight CSS animation: yellow background pulse (1s), then static yellow border. Uses @keyframes pulseHighlight. Auto-clears after 5s or on scroll
❌ Close X button in panel header, or click outside (backdrop), or press Escape. Slides back out (300ms)
