A

Individual Journal Lines

Show every debit/credit line. Most detailed. Good for auditors who need to trace every offset.

B

Journal Entries (Grouped)

One row per journal entry. Cleaner. Expand to see lines. Best for managers reviewing P&L.

C

Mixed: Journal + Bank

Everything that contributes to the account balance. Most complete, but more rows to scan.
